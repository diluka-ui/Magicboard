Magicboard is a futuristic Android keyboard designed for a smooth and customizable typing experience.

Features:
• Sinhala phonetic typing
• English typing
• Numbers and emoji
• Shift, Backspace, Space and Enter keys
• Customizable keyboard styles
• Background photo and color settings
• Touch effects
• Modern futuristic interface

Magicboard is free and open source software licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
